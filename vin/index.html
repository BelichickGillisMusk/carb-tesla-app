101 info ›

Which test?

2013+ diesel (or 2018+ alt fuel) usually HD-OBD at $119. 2012 and older diesel usually OVI smoke/opacity at $219. VIN year is a starting point — engine year on the tag wins if they disagree.

Deadlines

Passing tests may be submitted up to 90 days before the vehicle deadline. Confirm dates in CTC-VIS, not from this lookup.

TRUCRS / registry

The truck still has to be in the CARB inventory with the right VIN and engine family. A federal VIN decode does not register the vehicle for you.
