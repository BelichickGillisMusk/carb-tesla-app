Which test does my truck need?

2013 or newer diesel (or 2018+ alternative fuel)

Usually needs an HD-OBD scan with a CARB-certified device. The technician plugs into the truck's diagnostic port and submits the result. Market test price: $119.

2012 or older diesel

Usually needs an OVI smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of emissions controls. This is the mobile smoke-test equipment this site is built around. Market test price: $219.

2017 or older alternative fuel

May need the visual emissions-control inspection but not the diesel smoke opacity test. Dispatch confirms fuel type and records before booking.

Not sure?

Have the VIN, engine year, fuel type, plate, and compliance deadline ready — dispatch will route the truck as HD-OBD, OVI smoke, or visual inspection. Or use Find a Tester on the homepage to get your local team.

CARB-backed rules of thumb

  • OBD-equipped vehicles generally use a CARB-certified OBD test device.
  • 2012 and older diesel engines generally need smoke opacity testing plus a visual inspection.
  • Passing tests may be submitted up to 90 days before the vehicle compliance deadline.
  • Vehicle owners should always confirm due dates in CTC-VIS.

Pricing, scheduling, and logistics.

How much does mobile CARB testing cost?

Current market test pricing is $119 for HD-OBD testing and $219 for OVI smoke or opacity testing. Fleet routes and multi-truck yards may qualify for bundled scheduling.

Do you come to our yard or job site?

Yes. The whole network is built around mobile appointments at fleet yards, warehouses, contractor lots, farms, and job sites across California.

What information should I have ready before scheduling?

Have the truck VIN, license plate, engine year if known, odometer or hour meter, contact name, site address, and whether the truck is 2013 or newer for HD-OBD or 2012 and older for OVI smoke testing.

Can one appointment cover multiple trucks?

Yes. Multiple trucks at the same yard are preferred because they reduce travel time and lower the per-truck effort for everyone.

What happens if a truck is not ready when the technician arrives?

Dispatch asks for site details ahead of time so trucks can be parked, accessible, warmed up if needed, and ready for the right test type.

Which local team will I get?

City teams cover about 50 miles around Roseville, Stockton, Hayward, Fairfield, and Sacramento. San Diego is covered county-wide by Mobile CARB Testing — San Diego. Everywhere else, statewide OVI dispatch routes the closest tester. Use Find a Tester or ask the dispatch assistant in the corner of this page.
