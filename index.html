CARB-backed rules of thumb.

2013 or newer diesel (or 2018+ alternative fuel)

Usually needs an HD-OBD scan with a CARB-certified device — $119.

2012 or older diesel

Usually needs the OVI smoke/opacity test plus a visual inspection — $219.

Deadlines

Passing tests may be submitted up to 90 days before the vehicle compliance deadline. Always confirm due dates in CTC-VIS.
