What is the difference between HD-OBD and OVI smoke testing?

HD-OBD testing reads supported onboard diagnostic data, commonly for 2013 and newer diesel trucks. OVI smoke or opacity testing is used for older diesel equipment that needs a visible emissions opacity check.

Can one appointment cover multiple trucks?

Yes. Multiple trucks at the same yard are preferred because they reduce travel time and make pricing experiments in San Diego easier to compare against broader hub pages.
