# Blog footer block — paste at the bottom of every blog post (all sites)

Works on Squarespace (add a Code block) and any HTML blog. Same rules as the
sites: each satellite city covers a ~50-mile radius, San Diego coverage is
San Diego County only, and the app is the catch-all for everywhere else.
